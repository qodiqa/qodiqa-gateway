QODIQA
Execution Trust Infrastructure

Not AI chat. Not a gateway. Not observability. The layer that decides whether an AI execution is allowed to happen — and proves it afterward.

QODIQA verifies every AI execution before it happens, enforcing cryptographic integrity, policy compliance, consent validation, replay protection, transparency logging and immutable audit — while generating verifiable cryptographic evidence for every decision it makes.
